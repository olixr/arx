The World

The Road North

The Dawnlands hang eight towns on one long road, every one of them built, named and peopled by hand. The country between them is the real story: it comes in eleven grades, and the gazetteer has an honest word for each of them.

The Long Dark

The key is the dungeon

Dungeons in Arx are cut by their keys. A rift-cut key carries a place inside it: the same key always opens the same halls, and its ward holds three turns before it wears through. Six kinds of dark to cut into — cavern, mine, stronghold, crypt, warren, heartwood — with a champion standing between you and the chest.

Nothing respawns behind you. Clear a hall and it stays cleared until the key wears out.

The Dread Crown

The world crowns its foes

Eight crowned bosses hold ground out there, each with its own phases, its own summons, and a wind-up you can read if you keep your nerve. The bigger the telegraph, the harder it lands. When a crown falls, the whole field marks the felling.

309 abilities · no classes

Thirteen schools of light

Twenty-four skills climb to 99 by doing, two more keep themselves secret until your hands find them, and the deepest arts live in the weapons that teach them. Every sigil below is live geometry — drawn by the same code that draws it in a fight.

Hold the teaching blade and its art is on loan. Win with it, and the lesson sticks.

Field Notes

The rest of the ledger

Beastcraft

The wild at heel

Sixteen wild species will walk beside you once your beastcraft earns them — wolf, bear, lynx, great owl, worg among them. A companion fights at your side, levels through its own deeds, and falls without ever being lost.

Farming & building

The tended earth

Twenty-three crops, livestock that learn to trust your hands, and eighty buildable pieces that persist in the shared world. What you raise stays raised — and only you can pull your own walls down.

Reputation

Six banners keep books on you

Every faction runs its own ledger, from hunted to champion. Prices, fines, work and doors all follow the name you carry. “Walk on. The watch knows your face.”

The spoken land

188 voices

2,293 lines of dialogue, every character cast and voiced. Two of them may tell you different truths about the same thing. Leave with both.

Mounts

The road grows short

The Dawnlands courser, the shaggy Hoargate garron, the night sabercat. You ride to cross the world; the moment you fight, your boots are back on the ground.

The soundtrack

Eighteen tracks

Town themes, road songs, two kinds of night, and music that only plays when something with a crown has noticed you.

With Friends

One world, held in common

Arx is a single, persistent, shared world. Party up and walk the same road, or step into a friend’s live dungeon run mid-fight — the run is the owner’s, the door is open to the party. Ore veins deplete and come back for everyone. A house someone raised last winter is still standing when you walk past it tomorrow.

No download, no launcher, no spec sheet. A browser tab is the whole machine.
