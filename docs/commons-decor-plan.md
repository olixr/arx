# THE COMMONS — the general shelf every town owns

## Why

Two waves of town dressing (391–404) and two of trade dressing
(405–428) gave the streets their civic anchors and their working
trades — and left a hole exactly where the ORDINARY life goes. The
audit: every shipped piece is a SPECIALIST. There is no candle
burning on a stand anywhere in the kingdom, no lantern below the
LampPost's civic scale, no wayside faith, no statuary but one
founder, no stool, no settle, no basket, no jar, no game left
mid-move, no festival pole, no traveler's kit dropped by a door,
and nothing general for the water edge every town is built against
(the road-span law: every town touches water). This kit is the
GENERAL layer — twenty pieces chosen for REPEATED REUSE: each one
seats believably in at least four different scenes (inn, home,
shop, square, yard, shore), so the shelf multiplies every dressing
pass that comes after it.

## The kit's voice

TENDED, NEVER LEFT — the town voice, inherited whole. Every piece
is in USE: the candle burning, the game mid-move, the broom still
warm from the sweeping, the skiff hauled up but not abandoned, the
cloaks on the pegs because their owners are INSIDE. And PERIOD
TRUTH holds the door: if the smith can't forge it, the cooper
can't stave it, the mason can't lay it, or the carpenter can't
join it, the street doesn't own it. Every piece below passes.

## Laws this kit answers

- **BODY-RULER**: stools at the knee, settle back at the chest,
  guardian plinth+beast over the head, maypole the tallest timber
  on the green.
- **2.5D TOP-PLANE**: every flat — settle rail, plinth cap, sundial
  face, cask head, skiff gunwale — shows its foreshortened top.
- **THE BOUNDS ARE THE CANVAS**: stationBody derived from true
  painted extent + margin (the woodpile's axe is not lost twice).
- **ZERO LIGHT ENTRIES**: every flame in this kit is PAINT. The
  LampPost owns the town night — proven again by the night wide.
- **UNSIGNED HASH LAW**: every dealt index is `h >>> k`.
- **A LEAD COILS, NEVER LOOPS** (mooring post); **GLAZED WARE NEVER
  BARE CLAY** (jars); **A CASK LIES ON ITS BELLY** (tap cask);
  **FEET ON DIRT READ PALLET** (basket skid); **A SCOOP IS A
  VESSEL** (barrow shows its open box, never a blade).

### Audit verdicts (three paint passes, tier-0 field rig)

- **A SILHOUETTE IS A SUM OF READABLE CIRCLES** (the guardian):
  pass one drew the seated hound as one long path and shipped a
  SLUG. Rebuilt from composed masses — rump disc, chest disc,
  head disc, EAR TRIANGLES, muzzle wedge, straight leg columns —
  and the beast reads at every zoom. The wayshrine's keepsake
  hound reuses the same construction smaller.
- **A SHRINE IS A CAIRN SOMEBODY SQUARED**: the neat stone
  rectangle read as a WINDOW FRAME with a ghost. Rework: jittered
  boulder silhouette, a true ARCH of dark, the hound PALE against
  it, tilted capstone slab.
- **FOUR POSTS UNDER A BELLY READ AS LEGS** (the tap cask's
  barrel-pig verdict): a lying cask's stand must be JOINERY —
  plank cheeks, a visible bearer, splayed feet — and the tap
  must be a LONG spout (the short curl read as a pig's tail).
- **A FLAT RAIL OR IT'S A BARREL** (the settle): a rounded top
  rail ellipse turned the whole high-back into a barrel rim.
  Square beam, flat lit plane, planks parted by seams. And a
  thrown cloak drapes over the RAIL — on the arm it shrank to a
  door handle (pass three).
- **THE WHEEL STANDS PROUD** (the barrow): an edge-on wheel
  vanished and the box read as a bread trough. The wheel now
  rises above the box line, spoked and iron-shod, drawn LAST.
- **CLEAR AIR BETWEEN THE PIECES** (the wayfarer's kit): four
  props merged into one lump; the pack now stands UPRIGHT, the
  staff leans into empty sky, the roll and skin keep distance.
- **BRIGHT SHELF FOR SMALL CLOTH**: ribbons, cloaks, and throws
  deal from dye indices [1,2,3,4,5,9] only — linen and charcoal
  vanish against pole and oak at map scale.
- **A GAME IS A SQUARE, NOT A SPIRAL**: concentric grid ellipses
  on the round top read as a cinnamon roll; the tafl board is a
  ruled square laid on the round, pegs on intersections.
- **LIFT THE HULL OUT OF THE TAR**: the skiff's dark strakes
  merged into a gutter; warm oak courses with BOLD lap edges, a
  true sheer curve, and the one bright gunwale line made it a
  boat. Tar keeps only the garboard shadow.

### Rig lessons banked

- **/settile DOES NOT PERSIST a server restart** — the brush
  writes memory, not the DB. A restart wipes the staged pad;
  restage before any post-restart shot.
- **THE LOGIN INPUT KEEPS FOCUS** after Enter World, and
  typingCheck then eats every game key while chat still works
  (chat is DOM). reclaimFocus() politely refuses to steal from
  an input. Blur + canvas focus first, or drive the TOUCH
  LEVERS (`__arx.input.touchMoveX/touchAttack`) which bypass the
  keymap — though headless chromium never got frames consumed
  in this session's lane (the interactive shared browser is the
  smash rig of record; renderer.smashProp() drives the client
  wreck path deterministically for kit proofs).
- **THE ROSTER CARD HIDES A SECOND PASSWORD FIELD**: blind fills
  hit the classic form's input and re-trip the auth limiter.
  Click "Sign in with a username" first.
- **KILL BY PORT, NEVER BY PATTERN**: a `pkill -f "tsx
  src/index.ts"` took out a neighbor's lane-8 rig. `kill $(lsof
  -ti :PORT)` touches only your own.
- Tier-0 staging: probe `/wilds` for "tier 0" before padding —
  the near frontier's spiders webbed the first shoot to death.
