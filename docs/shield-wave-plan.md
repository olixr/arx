# THE WALL HAS TWELVE FACES — the shield wave

The offhand roster doubles: twelve new shields, every one an endgame-feeling
piece owned by a faction, a boss, or a craft pinnacle that already exists in
the world. The standing laws all hold: THE SHIELD IS A PLANE (one yaw law, no
per-facing art), EVERY SHIELD PAINTS ITSELF (a bespoke `sig` painter per
shield, no shared charges), FLAT LIKE EVERYTHING ELSE (flat fills, the budget:
field + ≤3 structural planes + one worked charge + the dish), and the
GREATSHIELD CLASS law — every tier-3+ shield is a NEW outline, pinned by test.

## The second rig — THE WALL CARRY

The roster splits into carry classes, a real rig fact, not a size:

- `fist` — buckler/round/targe/courtround: punched out, angled, lively.
- `arm`  — heater/kite/ribwall/reliquary/leaf: strapped, guarded at the chest.
- `wall` — tower and everything above it: the full-body class. New carriage law
  in `solveShield`:
  - **THE PLANT**: settled in guard, a wall grounds — tilt goes to zero, the
    face squares toward the threat, the whole plane rides lower so the bottom
    rim sits by the shin: a fortification, not a held prop. Idle sway is
    halved; a planted door does not breathe.
  - **THE SHOULDER**: running, the wall can't stay planted — it rises, cants
    into the stride, and turns edge-forward like a carried door.
  - **THE BASH**: the finisher drives it face-first, full-square, the one
    moment a wall attacks.
  All channels continuous; every existing test pin (upright, grip-behind,
  front-of-body, no ground drag, continuity) holds. Body attunement is by
  construction — the whole carriage is authored in body-height units and reads
  through `s`/`wS`, so every humanoid rig (and every size roll) holds its
  shield at its own scale; audited across bodies in wieldlab.

Design keys (inspiration honored, never copied — each remade in Arx's own
factions and the flat house dialect):

- **Lowhall breacher** — the Red Company's arguing door: dark lacquered boards,
  crowbar scars, an iron boot-plate heel, the company's red slash mark. A tool
  that got promoted.
- **Legion doorwall** — hobgoblin issue: a riveted blackened slab, two columns
  of forged diamond bosses, three punch spikes off the OUTER edge only (the
  shield-wall's free edge), one crimson campaign band.
- **Stagheart palisade** — hunter's wall: staked boards with three spike-cut
  tops in the outline, rawhide lashings, a tar antler-brow charge. The palisade
  you carry to the stand.
- **Fellhorn gate** — a barrow gate off its hinges: weathered pale planks,
  iron scroll-hinges at the shoulders, a fell-ram's skull chief with down-curled
  horns, the tar fell-mark chevron. Bone and iron, no brightwork.
- **Brinehold carapace** — the tide's rampart made bearable: scalloped shell
  wall in storm-green, kelp enamel bands, barnacle studs, a pearl boss.
- **Wintercourt rime** — the Court's token: pale birch-silver round, a
  court-silver crescent inlay, and a rime star of everfrost splinters struck
  from the center. Cold ceremony.
- **Nightveil pinion** — the veil's own wall: blued night-steel, the outer edge
  FLETCHED (three swept barb blades in the outline), a gilded rachis seam. The
  raptor's wing raised between you and the answer.
- **Vale reliquary** — the pilgrim way's silver: deep lake-indigo enamel under
  a worked moonsilver cross with flared finials and a moonstone at the
  crossing. Slender where the bulwark's brass is broad.
- **Cindermaw bulwark** — the Brand's furnace door: charred iron plates with
  live ember seams between them, a grate mouth at the heart, twin heat-cracked
  lugs. Everything bright on it is heat.
- **Everwood crest** — a silverbark leaf the length of a body: midrib spine,
  swept margins, imbued vein filigree in pale green-gold. The Everwood gives;
  no axe takes.
- **Gatefall bulwark** — riftward: an obelisk-cut wall in void-violet with a
  tall bezel of gate glass set through it, light splintering at its edges. It
  matches the gatefall gauntlets' glass.
- **Aldaren's Gate** — the capital carried: moonpale steel under a gold crown
  chief, three cascade stripes falling the full height — the crowned falls of
  Silverfall as heraldry. The biggest plane in the game (a hair past the
  aegis), and the top of the wall ladder.

## THE CARVED SHELL — the v3 substrate rebuild (as-built, 2026-08-17)

The pancake verdict: turned past the three-quarter, every shield came apart
into parallel sheets — face card, furniture card, crest card, each hovering in
its own air — because the face art projected through ONE flat affine onto the
plane tangent to the dome's crown, and every relief measured its height from
that same tangent plane. v3 makes the SURFACE the one truth. All of it lives in
`shields.ts`; `solveShield` (the carriage) is untouched and every one of its
pins held.

- **THE WRAPPED FACE.** The face art is painted once into a cached
  design-space canvas (`FACE_CACHE`, LRU 64, keyed by paint fields + lit side +
  resolution rung; `LIVING_SIGS` faces repaint per clock tick) and dealt onto
  the dome in vertical strips, each strip an affine seated at its own station's
  shell height (`domeU` waist profile). The near limb holds its width, the far
  limb foreshortens away, and past the shell's horizon a strip back-faces and
  is culled — the art rolls out of view the way paint on a curved board does.
  Seam law: strips overlap by ~1.25 DESTINATION px (a source-px overlap
  shrinks with the blit's downscale and let hairline gaps open).
- **THE SEATED FITTING.** `rPx`/`rPy` root relief height on the dome at the
  point's own station (`0.5 + curve·dome + h`), never on the crown plane. At
  the dome's heart the two agree exactly, so centered fittings kept their
  authored face-on seat to the pixel. Spikes bed at `0.5 + curve·0.3` — the
  binding's own shell height.
- **THE VISIBLE WINDOW.** The strip scan measures the surviving u-range and
  hands it to the relief pass (`ReliefCtx.uVis0/uVis1`). The solid vocabulary
  (prism/pyramid/polyAt/strokeAt/reliefShadow + the two custom-projection
  sites) culls whole elements that rolled past the horizon, and a convex-hull
  WINDOW CLIP confines partial elements to their visible half. Crests and
  spikes take the FULL window — protruding is their job.
- **THE PROUD PROFILE.** The `open < 0.07` early-return is dead: edge-on the
  bright crest line still finishes the slab, and boss, spikes and crests draw
  at EVERY yaw (pinned in shields.test.ts). Only the shield's back hides them.
- **THE TANGENT FALLBACK.** The old flat-affine face path survives for the
  node test harness (no DOM) and for shields under ~14 device px of
  half-height, where the warp is sub-pixel; the painters' own grazing gates
  still guard that road (they read the real frame, not the cache's synthetic
  open-1 frame, so no painter changed).

Proven on the shield sheet (all 23 rows × 6 yaw stations × hero + 3 gameplay
scales, before/after), on the wield sheet rows 8-9 and 36-39 (board + wall
carries, all 8 facings), and the living clock verified by differing captures
at two freeze times. tsc clean, 666/666 client tests.
